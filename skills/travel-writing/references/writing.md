# Evidence-aware travel writing

Order records by their original timestamps. Prefer photo taken time over upload time. Treat nearby timestamps or coordinates as clues, not proof. Attach relative source paths to factual itinerary bullets.

Use these confidence labels when useful:

- Confirmed: directly supported by a post, note, metadata, or visible photo content.
- Inferred: supported by adjacent records but not directly stated.
- Unknown: a gap or contradiction requiring the user's memory.

Preserve the user's voice without inventing quotations. Do not fabricate transport, meals, companions, reactions, place names, or chronology. Put unresolved details in a questions section. Link only selected media in the final draft; keep evidence citations in working files unless requested for publication.
